# PINN Model for Dark Matter Detection

A research-grade Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) model implemented in PyTorch to dark matter parameter estimation.

## 📊 Results & Figures
Here is a preview of the model training evolution, quantitative performance evaluations, and final predictions compared with experimental data:

* **Flux Spectrum Comparison & Loss Convergence:** Visualizes the model's fitted flux spectrum, loss landscapes, and training function convergence.
* **PINN vs. DNN (Interpolation & Extrapolation):** Demonstrates superior physical generalization and robustness of the PINN architecture compared to standard DNNs outside training domains.
* **Quantitative Performance Evaluation (MSE):** Highlights lower Mean Squared Errors across both interpolation and extrapolation regimes.
